Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar feature that keeps data on your device. This page lists what {legal.siteName} uses and how you can control it. For everything else about your data, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## What this site stores itself

{legal.siteName} doesn't set any tracking cookies of its own. It saves a few settings in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your choices. They never leave your device:

| Name | What it's for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Your light or dark mode choice | Until you clear it |
| `likedPosts` | Posts you've liked, so the heart stays filled | Until you clear it |
| `savedPosts` | Posts you've saved to read later | Until you clear it |
| `viewedPosts` | When you last opened each post, so a view is counted at most once every 48 hours | 48 hours per post |

These are needed for the features you use, so they don't require consent.

## Advertising cookies

Ads on this site are provided by **Google AdSense**, and they're what keeps {legal.siteName} free and online. Google and its partners may set cookies (such as `IDE`, `__gads` and `__gpi`) to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance and, if you allow it, personalize ads based on your visits here and to other sites.

- Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later.
- Turn off personalized ads anywhere in [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- See [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Analytics cookies

The site uses **Google Tag Manager** to load Google's measurement tools, such as **Google Analytics**. These may set cookies (such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`, kept for up to 2 years) to count visits and see which pages are read, using data like your approximate location, device and browser. They don't identify you by name.

- Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent first.
- You can opt out of Google Analytics with [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking advertising cookies won't stop the site working; you'll just see non-personalized ads. Clearing local storage resets your theme and your liked and saved posts.
